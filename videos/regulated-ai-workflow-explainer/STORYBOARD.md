- Use deterministic GSAP timelines only.
- Transitions are clean wipes and connector handoffs, not glitch effects.
- Status colors carry meaning: green for permitted, red for stopped, amber for human review, teal for trusted control-plane routing.
- Narration clips define the scene rhythm; visual holds provide breathing room without extending beyond 60 seconds.
