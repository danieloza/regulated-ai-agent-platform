# Regulated AI Workflow Explainer

## Objective

Explain how the platform converts an AI request into an evidence-backed authorization decision. The film is a portfolio and stakeholder asset, not a claim of automatic deployment or production certification.

## Audience

- Engineering and platform leaders
- Security, risk, and compliance stakeholders
- Technical recruiters evaluating enterprise AI-governance work

## Format

- 1920 × 1080, 16:9
- 55–60 seconds
- English voice-over only
- Clean, deterministic local render with no watermark
- GitHub and LinkedIn friendly

## Creative Direction

The request moves through an “evidence corridor.” Every stage either binds approved evidence, narrows authority, or stops execution. The visual language should feel like a premium security control plane: dark navy surfaces, precise grids, restrained glow, readable status colors, and high-information motion.

## Required Claims

- Answers are grounded in approved evidence.
- Policy outcomes are allowed, denied, or approval required.
- Tools receive exact scope, with no shell or direct database credentials.
- Sensitive writes require independent human approval.
- Policy Replay and Security Twin evaluate historical regressions and modeled blast radius.
- Release Assurance produces an evidence-backed GO or NO-GO recommendation.
- GO is authorization evidence, not automatic deployment.

## Boundaries

- Do not imply the platform deploys changes automatically.
- Do not claim formal certification or production readiness.
- Do not show secrets, real customer data, or local filesystem paths.
- Do not use stock people, humanoid robots, fake terminals, or decorative code.
